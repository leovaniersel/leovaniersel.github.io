About

My research develops mathematical theory and practical software for reconstructing and analysing phylogenetic trees and networks. After studying applied mathematics at the University of Twente, I obtained my PhD at Eindhoven University of Technology. After that, I worked as a postdoc at the University of Canterbury, as a teacher in primary and secondary schools in Tanzania and Kenya, and as a researcher at CWI in Amsterdam. I joined TU Delft in 2014.
