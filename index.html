About

After studying applied mathematics at the University of Twente, I obtained my PhD at Eindhoven University of Technology. After that, I worked as a postdoc at the University of Canterbury, as a teacher in primary and secondary schools in Tanzania and Kenya, and as a researcher at CWI in Amsterdam. In 2014, I joined TU Delft, where I’m currently associate professor. I’m co-chair of AIM.
